Species Spotlight 1

CENDRAWASIH

English: Bird of Paradise

🦚

Physical Characteristics

Vibrant yellow, red, blue, and green feathers. Male birds possess elongated decorative tail plumes.

Habitat

Dense tropical rainforests of Papua, eastern Indonesia, and surrounding island canopy levels.

Food & Diet

Omnivorous diet consisting of rainforest fruits, berries, insects, spiders, and small frogs.

Behavior & Courtship

Males perform elaborate dance displays on tree branches to attract females, fluffing their vibrant feathers.

Conservation Status

Protected / Threatened Protected under Indonesian law due to historical poaching for feathers and deforestation.

Species Spotlight 2

BALI STARLING

Indonesian Name: Jalak Bali

🕊️

Physical Characteristics

Pure snowy white body, bare electric-blue skin patch around eyes, black tail tips, and a delicate head crest.

Habitat

Endemic exclusively to the dry savanna forest and lowland trees of West Bali National Park.

Food & Diet

Insects, caterpillars, ants, small fruits, seeds, and wild nectar.

Behavior & Chirping

Monogamous birds that live in pairs or small flocks, producing clear melodic chirping sounds.

Conservation Status

Critically Endangered Heavily impacted by illegal pet trading; saved via active breeding programs.

Text 1 • Grade 9 Level

THE BEAUTIFUL CENDRAWASIH

The Cendrawasih, commonly known in English as the Bird of Paradise, is one of the most magnificent wild birds found in Indonesia. They primarily inhabit the dense tropical rainforests of Papua and surrounding eastern islands. Their exotic appearance makes them famous worldwide.

Male Cendrawasih birds are especially famous for their brilliant and colorful plumage. Their feathers showcase shades of bright yellow, deep crimson red, emerald green, and rich blue. Furthermore, adult males have long, delicate wire-like tail feathers. During mating season, male birds perform unique courtship dances on forest branches, swaying and displaying their colorful feathers to attract females.

Cendrawasih birds feed mainly on tropical fruits, berries, insects, and small tree frogs. Unfortunately, their beauty has threatened their survival. Historical hunting for decorative feather trade and continuous forest deforestation have reduced their natural population. Today, Cendrawasih is strictly protected under Indonesian conservation laws to preserve them for future generations.

Checking Comprehension: Text 1

1. Where is the main natural habitat of the Cendrawasih?

2. Why do male Cendrawasih perform courtship dances?

Text 2 • Grade 9 Level

THE AMAZING BALI STARLING

The Bali Starling, locally named Jalak Bali (Leucopsar rothschildi), is an endangered bird endemic exclusively to the island of Bali, Indonesia. Recognized as Bali's official bird mascot, it lives in dry monsoon forests within the protected sanctuary of West Bali National Park.

Visually, the Bali Starling is strikingly distinctive. Almost its entire body is covered in clean, snow-white feathers, accented by black tips on its wing and tail feathers. Around its eyes, it features a bare patch of vibrant sky-blue skin. Both male and female starlings have a stylish drooping feather crest on top of their heads.

In the wild, Bali Starlings feed on caterpillars, insects, seeds, and small wild fruits. Because of illegal poaching for pet collection and narrow habitat availability, their numbers dropped drastically in the late 20th century. Thanks to active breeding sanctuaries and strict protection, this magnificent bird is being saved from total extinction.

Checking Comprehension: Text 2

1. What color is the skin patch around the Bali Starling's eyes?

2. True or False: The Bali Starling is found naturally in all regions of Asia.
